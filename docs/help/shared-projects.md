A **shared project** is run by one business with another taking part. Each business keeps its own books. The host shares the project's tasks and comments; time, rates, invoices and payments never cross between businesses.

## What is shared

The host's workspace owns the project. A partner sees the tasks the host already shows its client, and the comments written into the shared conversation. A partner never sees the host's rates, hours, invoices, files, internal notes or other projects, and the host never sees the partner's.

A task the host has hidden from its own client stays internal to the host. A partner who is not an owner or admin of its workspace sees only the tasks assigned to them.

## Inviting a partner

A host's owner or admin opens a project, chooses **Sharing with other workspaces** and enters the address of the person to invite. SVC gives a link to send them. Nothing is shared until they accept. The link works once, for the person it was written to, and expires after 14 days. You can withdraw an invitation before it is accepted.

## Accepting

The invited person signs in, sees the invitation under **Invited to a shared project** when they choose a workspace, and picks which of their workspaces joins. Only a workspace they own or administer is offered. They can decline instead.

## Working together

Everyone who can see a task can read its comments and add their own. A comment written here is never shown to the host's client. The host can hand a task to people of its own and of each partner; each person is shown with the workspace they work for.

## Your own books

A partner links one of its own projects to the shared one. Time is logged against that project, in the partner's workspace, under the partner's own agreement and rates. The host never reads the entries, their rates or their amounts.

## Leaving or removing

A partner's owner or admin can leave at any time; the host's owner or admin can remove a partner. Access ends at once, the partner's people come off the tasks, and the partner keeps its own project, time and invoices. Comments already written stay in the thread.

## Joining without a workspace

If you have no workspace of your own when you accept an invitation, one is made for you: a light workspace with you as its owner. You name the client you bill for the work; it holds that client and a project linked to the shared one, and nothing of the host's. See [My work](help:my-work).
