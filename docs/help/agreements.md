A proposal is an offer. An agreement is the standing terms once the client accepts.

## Retainer

A **Retainer** is a block of hours the client pays for each period, used or not.

## Rollover

**Rollover** carries unused retainer hours into the next period, up to the limit on the agreement.

## Overage

**Overage** is work beyond the retainer, billed at the hourly rate.

## Recurring services

Hosting and similar services are charged on their own cadence, separate from hours.
