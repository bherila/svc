The **approved timesheet** is the hours someone else approved, for one calendar month: who worked each, what the client reads, and who approved it, how and when. It is what you invoice from, or send to your client.

## What is on it

Only hours a reviewer, or the client's own contact, approved. Draft hours, hours that are only submitted, disputed hours, and hours that count as approved only because an invoice was paid are not on it. It shows no rate and none of your private notes.

An owner or admin sees the whole workspace's hours. Anyone else sees their own.

## Getting it out

Use **Download CSV** to open it in a spreadsheet, or **Download PDF** to send it as it is. Both list the same rows as the page, with the month's total. The month is the calendar month the work was done in, whatever your billing cadence is.

## Moving between months

Use **Previous** and **Next**. A month that has nothing approved yet says so; hours appear here as soon as they are approved.
