The overview is where a client starts. It uses the same key figures and chart as the [dashboard](help:revenue-overview), for one client.

## Key figures and chart

MRR, year to date, trailing 12 months and open balance, with a 24-month chart and the same basis and expense toggles as the dashboard. The figures are only visible to owners and admins.

## This period

Retainer hours used against the hours included in the current billing period, for each agreement in force. It loads a moment after the page.

## Agreements

Each active agreement is a card with its cadence, retainer, recurring services and end date. Open one to edit its terms.

## Invoices, projects and proposals

The five most recent invoices, with a link to all of them. Each project has a **Log time** link that opens the time sheet on that project. Recent proposals link to their pages.

Drafting and sending proposals, activating agreements, and billing schedules are on the client's [Agreements](help:agreements) tab. Drafting an invoice is on its Invoices tab.
