An expense is a cost passed through to the client.

## Receipts

Attach the receipt to the expense. Approved expenses with receipts go on the next invoice.

## Repeating expenses

A schedule creates the same expense each period, so recurring costs are not typed again.

## Ready to bill and the client's approval

Approving an expense in the workspace means it is ready to bill. It is a check that the receipt is right and the expense belongs to this client. The client approves separately in their portal, or by paying the invoice it is on; that does not change what is billed. Nobody's approval of their own expense counts for the client.
