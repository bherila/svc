My work is your own page: the tasks assigned to you, a quick way to log time, and what you have logged so far. It shows nothing about money. You never see rates, amounts or invoices here, and nobody else's time.

## This week

Your open tasks across every client, grouped by when they are due: overdue first, then today, later this week, later, and tasks with no date. Open a task to read it. If you manage the project the task belongs to, you can also change its status from the list.

## Quick log

Pick the project, the date and how long you worked, and say what you did. Type the time any way you like: 2:30, 2.5, 2h30m or 150m all mean the same. The list offers only projects you can log time on. If it is empty, ask whoever runs the workspace to add you to a project as a contributor.

## My time

What you logged this week and this month, by project, with where each part of it is in review. See [time](help:time#how-time-moves-through-review) for what each state means.
