**Reviewer access** lets someone outside your business approve the hours and expenses you bill one client for. It is how a client pays two businesses and asks one to vouch for the other: each business keeps its own books, and the client decides in one place.

## Giving access

An owner or admin opens **Reviewers** in the account menu, chooses a client, and names who reviews: one of that client's contacts, or a workspace you already share a project with. Access can cover all of the client's projects or only one. Nothing is shared until you give it.

A contact is reached by the email address on their record. A workspace is reached through its owners and admins. Your own team cannot be given access, because a client approves, not your staff. Someone who already has portal access to the client is decided by that access instead.

## What a reviewer sees

Only that client's hours and expenses that are ready to bill, or that the person who did the work has submitted for review: date, time, the description the client already sees, and the project. An expense shows its amount, because that is what is being approved. A reviewer never sees rates, invoices, agreements, internal notes or any other client.

Approving or disputing records the decision in your workspace, against your own entries, with the reviewer's name. You see it on the entry like any client decision. Nobody can approve their own hours or expenses, whatever access they hold.

A one-person business has nobody to approve its hours, so it submits them instead (see [submitting hours and expenses](help:submitting-hours)). Submitted rows reach the reviewer without anyone in the business approving them, and the reviewer's approval is what makes them approved.

## Taking access back

Choose **Take back…** beside a grant. It stops at once: the reviewer's list empties on their next click, and any approval they already gave stays. Every grant, take-back, view and decision is written to your workspace's access history.

## The To approve list

**To approve** lists every provider that asked you to decide, one card each, with the hours and expenses grouped by calendar month. It looks and works like the list on your portal home. Approve a month, approve single rows, or dispute a row with a note. A provider appears because you are a billing contact of its client, or because it gave you reviewer access; a revoked grant is simply gone.
