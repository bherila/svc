The roster is one row per client with the numbers that matter. Owners and admins see revenue; other members see a plain list of the clients they work with.

## Reading a row

- **MRR** is recurring revenue per month, as on the [dashboard](help:revenue-overview#the-key-figures).
- **YTD** is revenue since January 1. **TTM** is the last twelve full months plus this month so far, drawn as a small trend line.
- **Open / overdue** is what the client owes and how much of it is past due.
- **Agreement health** is a meter of retainer hours used against the hours included this period. It appears a moment after the table, because it reads each agreement's hours. A badge says when an agreement ends within 60 days or the client has used more than its retainer.

## Finding clients

Search by name, filter to active clients, agreements ending soon, clients over their retainer or clients with overdue invoices, and sort by any figure. The totals row adds up the clients you are looking at.

## On a phone

Each client becomes a card with its name, MRR, open balance and retainer meter.
