The portal shows what you owe, how your retainer is being used and what needs your approval.

## What you owe

Open invoices are listed with the balance and due date. Open one to pay it.

## Approving time

Time your provider has reviewed waits for your approval. Approve it, or dispute it with a note.

## Your retainer

Your agreement shows the hours included each period and what has been used.

## Proposals

A proposal waits for your signature. Accepting it creates the agreement.
