The dashboard is where a workspace starts. Owners and admins see the money first (see [revenue overview](help:revenue-overview)), then a list of what needs you now, newest trouble first. Other members see only their own work.

## What needs you

Each row is one thing to do, with a link that goes straight to it.

- Invoices that are overdue, or drafts ready to issue.
- Time waiting for approval, grouped by client.
- Proposals sent and waiting for a signature.
- Billing schedules that are due to generate.

When nothing needs you, the list says so and shows nothing else.

## Why a row is here

A row stays until the thing it points at is done. Issue the draft, approve the time or record the payment and the row goes away on the next visit. See [time](help:time#how-time-moves-through-review) for how time is reviewed.
