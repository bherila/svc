**Submitting** tells whoever pays for your work, or vouches for it, that this month's hours and expenses are ready for them to look at. It is how a one-person business gets its time in front of a client or a reviewer without approving its own hours: you submit, someone else approves.

## What you can submit

Your own draft hours and expenses, one calendar month at a time. An hour needs the line the client reads (add it when you log the time) and has to be billable; a reviewer sees only that line, never your private note. Hours that are not billable, direct subcontractor time, and anything already marked ready to bill or billed are not waiting on you.

Choose **Submit** beside a row, or **Submit** for the month to send everything that can go. A row that cannot be submitted says why.

## While it is submitted

The reviewer sees the row in their **To approve** list. You cannot edit or delete it while it is there: withdraw it first, change it, and submit it again. Your own workspace cannot approve it either; that is the reviewer's decision, not yours.

## Taking it back

**Withdraw** removes a submission from the reviewer's list at once. You can withdraw until someone approves it. After that the approval stands, and it appears on your [approved timesheet](help:approved-timesheet).

## If it is disputed

A reviewer who disputes a row sends it back with a note, which you see beside it. Withdraw it, fix what the note asks, and submit it again.
